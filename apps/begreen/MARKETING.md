# Marketing BeGreen

## 1. The one-liner

> **BeGreen: do something green, prove it in 5 seconds, see what your friends did.**

Tagline: **Proof, not promises.**
Alternates for ads: *Do it. Prove it. Post it.* · *Post yours to see theirs.* · *Your city, decoded.*

## 2. The insight we sell on

Teenagers don't lack climate awareness. They're tired of it: endless doom, preachy campaigns, adults performing virtue online. What they lack is a **small, social, visible way to act** that doesn't feel cringe.

BeGreen turns sustainability from a *topic* into a *daily social ritual*:

- **It's social first.** You open it to see what your friends did, the way you'd open any social app. The good habit is the price of entry: *post yours to see theirs.*
- **It's anti-greenwashing by design.** A 5-second proof clip plus 3 friend verifications means nobody can fake it. That gives it credibility with a generation allergic to fake.
- **It's local and real.** “Air is poor in Milano today” hits harder than “the planet is warming”. The Drop gives everyone in town the same mission on the same day, which makes a shared moment like BeReal's, minus the panic.

**Positioning:** BeReal's authenticity + Strava's streaks and leagues + a local environmental dashboard. Not an eco app; a social app whose currency is green acts.

## 3. Who it's for

| Segment | Why they care | What we give them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Teens 14–19 (primary)** | Friends, status, streaks, a low-effort way to feel they're doing something | The feed, the Drop, streaks, badges, the city league |
| **Teachers and schools** | Civic and environmental education goals, projects that need real data | “Class” explanations, squad quests, the Community Report |
| **Associations** (Legambiente, WWF Young, clean-up groups) | Turnout, visibility, proof of impact for funders | Association squads, public posts, exportable reports |
| **Comuni / municipalities** | Engaging youth, participation data, environmental plans (PAES/PAESC) | Aggregated, anonymised participation data next to local air, water and nature data |

Teens are the users; schools and Comuni are the distribution and, later, the revenue.

## 4. Brand voice

- **Short, dry, a little funny.** “told mom no lift today. the 90 bus is lowkey a vibe.” Never “Save the planet! 🌍”.
- **Never guilt-trip.** We celebrate doing, never shame not-doing. No CO₂ doom counters.
- **Honest about data.** We say “model”, “estimate”, “citizen science”. Being transparent is part of being cool for this audience.
- **Visual identity:** black on white, Geist Medium wordmark, Geist Mono for the details. Looks like a fashion brand, not an NGO. No leaves-on-gradients, no stock earth globes.

## 5. Go-to-market: city by city, school by school

BeReal grew campus by campus; BeGreen grows **school by school, city by city**. Network effects are local (friends, class, city league), so we concentrate instead of spreading thin.

### Phase 0: seed one city (Milano), 6 weeks
- **10 launch schools** recruited through teachers already running civic or environmental projects. Each class becomes a squad with a launch quest (“Car-free week”).
- **Student Ambassadors** (2 per school): early access, exclusive black-and-white merch, a seat on the “BeGreen Council” that chooses special Drops.
- **City League launch**: a live leaderboard of schools, shown at a final event with the Comune. The Municipio presents the result together with real air-quality data.

### Phase 1: make it viral, months 2–4
- **Shareable proof clips.** The photo + 5 s proof tile exports as a 9:16 Story/TikTok with the BeGreen watermark: the product is its own ad.
- **Weekly “Green Wrapped”**: an auto-generated recap (“You avoided ≈4 kg CO₂e, top 12% in your school”), designed to be shared.
- **City Drops**: city-wide special Drops tied to real events (smog alerts, World Water Day, the first warm Saturday → park clean-up). Push: *“Air is Poor in Milano. Today's Drop is live.”*
- **Creator challenges** with mid-size local creators (cycling, thrifting, food, outdoor): *#BeGreenDrop*. We pay for participation, not for pretend green.

### Phase 2: expand, months 4–12
- Replicate the playbook in Torino, Bologna, Roma, Napoli, each through a partner Comune and 10 schools.
- Interschool **national league**, with a finals event.
- Partnerships with associations for public squads (“Plastic Free Navigli”), whose clean-ups become mass Drops.

## 6. Growth loops built into the product

1. **Unlock loop:** friends' posts are blurred → you post → you get notified when friends verify → you come back.
2. **Verification loop:** each proof needs 3 friends to verify → it pulls friends in and drives invites.
3. **Squad loop:** a quest needs N acts → classmates nudge each other → the whole class installs.
4. **League loop:** school vs. school → status and local press → more schools join.
5. **Report loop:** the Community Report goes to the Comune → the Comune promotes BeGreen to more schools.

## 7. Launch checklist

- App Store name: **BeGreen: Prove Your Green**. Subtitle: *Daily green drops with friends*.
- First 3 screenshots: the proof tile, the Drop, the city league. Never a dashboard first.
- Preview video: 15 s of fast cuts of real proof clips (bike, bin, refill), ending on *Proof, not promises.*
- Privacy-first messaging for parents and schools: approximate location only, no exact spots on posts, data shared with Comuni only aggregated and anonymised. Under-14s need parental consent.

## 8. What we measure

| Metric | Target at 3 months in the seed city |
| --- | --- |
| D1 / D7 / D30 retention | 55% / 35% / 20% |
| Proofs per weekly active user | ≥ 3 |
| Verification rate (proofs reaching 3 verifications) | ≥ 70% |
| Share rate (proofs exported to Stories/TikTok) | ≥ 15% |
| Schools active in the league | 25 |
| Comuni receiving reports | 3 |

North-star metric: **verified green acts per week.** It measures participation without overclaiming impact.

## 9. Money (without selling out)

- **Free for teens, always.** No ads from polluting industries, ever, and we say so publicly.
- **Schools and Comuni:** a paid “Community” tier with a live dashboard, report history, custom quests, and exports for environmental plans and grant applications.
- **Sponsored Drops**, only from vetted partners and only for verifiable acts (e.g. a bike-share free ride for a car-free Drop). Every sponsor is labelled.
- **BeGreen+:** cosmetic extras (profile themes, Wrapped styles). It never buys points or ranking.

## 10. Risks and how we handle them

| Risk | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Fake proofs | 5 s video + 3 peer verifications + flagging; later, AI checks on the backend |
| “Cringe eco app” perception | Social-first design, fashion-grade visuals, a humorous voice, ambassadors chosen by students |
| Overclaiming impact (greenwashing) | Every impact figure labelled as an estimate; source badges on all data; north-star metric is acts, not tonnes |
| Minors' privacy | Approximate location, no public exact spots, aggregation before sharing, parental consent under 14 |
| Novelty fading | Daily Drops from changing local data, seasonal leagues, city events |
